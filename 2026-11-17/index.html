The board opened an independent review of whether he had been targeted by Chinese intelligence, then met again after further allegations involving an employee. Beijing calls the spying claim baseless. The board says it has no reason to believe national security was compromised.

    SerenaThe chief financial officer, Colette Kress, is interim chief executive. Nvidia has its first board chair. And it reports earnings today.

    DanielWhy this is an AI story. Nearly every frontier model is trained on Nvidia chips, and Huang was the loudest voice in Washington for selling them to China. The stock is down about thirty percent from its October record. One point seven trillion dollars.

    SerenaThe tributes all told the same story: in 2012 the first big neural network was trained on two Nvidia gaming cards, and Huang bet the company on what that meant.

  3. Transcript · 1:07

    SerenaStory two. The same afternoon, a former OpenAI researcher named Will Depue published what he calls conclusive forensic evidence about China's four leading AI labs.

    DanielHere's the trick. Before a model is trained, its numbers are filled in at random. That random pattern depends on the exact chip that generated it, and traces survive training. Depue searched six trillion combinations and matched six Chinese models to Blackwell, Nvidia's newest chip, which can't legally be exported to China.

    SerenaHe then proved it on real hardware: a Blackwell chip reproduced one DeepSeek model's starting numbers exactly. An older chip reproduced none.

    DanielA former White House AI adviser called it stronger than DNA evidence. But read what it proves. It shows which chip started the model, not where that chip sat. Renting Blackwells in another country may be legal. And a critic points out that starting a model on a chip isn't the same as training it there.

    SerenaDepue's answer: wherever labs have disclosed their hardware, the fingerprint matched. Twenty-four out of twenty-four.

    SerenaStory three. Anthropic launched the roadshow for its initial public offering on Tuesday. Seventy to seventy-eight dollars a share, eight hundred twenty-five million shares.

    DanielAt the top of the range that raises about sixty-four billion dollars and values the company at as much as one point four trillion. It is expected to price tomorrow and trade Thursday. Individuals can request shares through Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood and others.

    SerenaThen people watched the video. One slide shows how much less computing it takes to train a model over time, on a curve that bends upward. A former employee wrote that the slide was a big factor in the decision to leave.

    DanielA computer scientist read the footnote. The placement of the data points, it says, is solely for illustrative purposes.

    SerenaThe investor David Sacks, who advises the White House on AI, said Anthropic is telling investors that AI improving itself has begun, and called that the most dangerous thing anybody can be working on. And a policy lawyer noted that Accenture, the firm Anthropic is paying to be its outside safety evaluator, appears in the video as a customer.

  5. Transcript · 1:58

    DanielStory four. Last week an outside audit said AI has doubled the pace of AI research at Anthropic. This week the forecasters did the arithmetic.

    SerenaFirst, the model. Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 last Monday. One demonstration: a thousand copies working together wrote a machine-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in thirty hours. Anthropic also says it deliberately held back the skills that would most speed up AI research itself.

    DanielTuesday, the authors of the AI 2027 forecast updated it. All six now expect an AI better than the best human AI researchers in 2027. Daniel Kokotajlo's words: I am scared, sad, and angry. Another researcher put the chance of superintelligence within a year at one in three.

    SerenaThe podcaster Dwarkesh Patel published his own version: AI went from failing arithmetic to solving a famous open problem in three years, and AI research is the same kind of search. His median is 2028.

    DanielThen the philosopher David Chalmers asked the useful question: how exactly does a two-times speedup get you there? Suppose intelligence has an easy part, coding and running experiments, and a hard part, call it research judgment. The easy part could explode while the hard part barely moves. He calls that a sub-explosion.

    SerenaAnd one chart for the skeptics: measured across every lab, the frontier is improving at about the rate it has been. What changed is who's in front.

    SerenaProfessor?

    StevenI can't tell you whether it's 2027. Neither can they. What I can tell you is how to listen. Every one of these forecasts is a model with an assumption carrying the weight. Chalmers found it: does the hard part speed up too? When someone hands you a projection, find the assumption before you look at the number.
